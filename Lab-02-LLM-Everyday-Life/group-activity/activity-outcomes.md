# Activity Outcomes – The Dehydrated Student

## 1. Identified Problem

Students often spend several hours attending lectures, working in laboratories, or studying without drinking enough water.

## 2. Project Goal

To develop an innovative product that encourages students to maintain healthy hydration habits throughout the day.

## 3. Target Users

- University students
- College students
- Students attending long lectures
- Students working in laboratories

## 4. Product Concept

The group will develop an innovative hydration-support product using Large Language Models (LLMs) as product-development partners.

## 5. Key Features

The final product features will be documented after the group completes the product development process.

## 6. Visual Prototype

The product mock-up will be added after finalizing the design.

## 7. Expected Outcome

A creative and practical solution that helps students become more aware of their daily water intake and develop better hydration habits.
