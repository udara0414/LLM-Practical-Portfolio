# Discussion Summary – Group 4

## Challenge
The Dehydrated Student

## 1. Problem Discussion

University students often forget to drink enough water because of their busy academic schedules, long lectures, laboratory sessions, and study activities.

## 2. Use of Large Language Models

Large Language Models (LLMs) were used as product-development partners to support the brainstorming and idea-generation process.

## 3. Product Development Process

The group explored possible solutions to encourage students to maintain better hydration habits.

The LLM supported the group in:
- Brainstorming innovative product ideas.
- Identifying possible product features.
- Defining target users.
- Exploring possible design concepts.
- Improving the overall product concept.

## 4. Critical Evaluation

The group reviewed and evaluated the suggestions generated by the LLM and considered their practicality and relevance to the identified problem.

## 5. Final Product

The final product concept, selected features, and visual prototype will be documented after completing the group activity.

## 6. Conclusion

LLMs supported the product-development process by providing ideas and helping the group explore possible solutions to an everyday student challenge.
