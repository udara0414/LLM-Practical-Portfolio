# Group Activity – Sipwise Product Development

## 1. Introduction

This section documents the group activity completed for Lab Sheet 2 – AI in Everyday Challenges under the module PUSL 3205 – Large Language Models and Generative AI.

For this activity, the lecturer assigned different everyday challenges to each group. Our group, **Group 4**, was assigned the challenge titled **"The Dehydrated Student."**

Based on the assigned challenge, our task was to explore how Large Language Models (LLMs) could be used as product-development partners to develop a potential solution.

Through group discussions and LLM-assisted brainstorming, we developed a product concept called **Sipwise**.

## 2. Assigned Challenge – The Dehydrated Student

The assigned challenge focused on university students who often forget to drink enough water because of their busy academic schedules.

Students may spend several hours attending lectures, laboratory sessions, and studying without taking sufficient breaks to drink water.

Although traditional reminder applications can send notifications at regular intervals, these reminders may arrive during lectures or other academic activities, making them inconvenient.

The challenge encouraged our group to think about a more suitable solution that considers students' daily routines and academic timetables.

## 3. Activity Objective

The main objective was to use an LLM as a product-development partner to transform the assigned everyday challenge into a practical product concept.

The activity involved:

- Understanding and analysing the assigned challenge.
- Identifying the needs and difficulties of the target users.
- Using an LLM to explore possible product ideas.
- Developing and refining a suitable product concept.
- Identifying potential LLM-based functionalities.
- Designing a visual prototype.
- Preparing a group presentation to communicate the proposed solution.

## 4. Proposed Product – Sipwise

**Sipwise** is a proposed AI-assisted hydration support product designed to help university students maintain better hydration habits while managing their academic schedules.

**Tagline:**

"Drink water on your class schedule, not when you're already thirsty."

The proposed solution combines two main components:

### 4.1 AI-Powered Mobile Application

The mobile application is designed to provide:

- Timetable-based hydration planning.
- Daily hydration progress tracking.
- Personalized hydration goals.
- Manual drink logging.
- AI-generated notifications.
- Campus water refill point discovery.
- Friends' hydration streaks.

### 4.2 ESP32-Powered Smart Bottle Cap

The proposed smart cap is designed to attach to a regular water bottle.

Its planned functionalities include:

- Sensor-based sip counting.
- ESP32-based hardware control.
- Bluetooth Low Energy communication.
- Blue and amber LED indicators.
- Left-behind bottle alerts.

## 5. Role of LLMs in Product Development

During the activity, our group explored how an LLM could support the product-development process.

The LLM was considered for:

1. **Idea Generation:** Exploring possible solutions to the assigned dehydration challenge.
2. **Feature Development:** Identifying and refining useful product features.
3. **Product Concept Development:** Exploring how a mobile application and smart hardware could work together.
4. **AI Functionality Identification:** Identifying potential applications of LLMs within the proposed product.
5. **Concept Refinement:** Improving and organizing the product idea based on the group's requirements.

The group discussed and selected relevant ideas before developing the final Sipwise concept.

## 6. Group Activity Process

The group followed these main stages:

1. **Challenge Analysis:** Studied the lecturer-assigned challenge, "The Dehydrated Student."
2. **Problem Understanding:** Discussed why university students may forget to drink water.
3. **Brainstorming:** Used LLM assistance to explore potential product solutions.
4. **Product Development:** Developed the Sipwise concept and identified its main features.
5. **LLM Integration:** Identified suitable LLM-based functionalities.
6. **Prototype Design:** Prepared a visual mobile application mock-up using Figma.
7. **Presentation Preparation:** Organized the proposed solution and its features for the group presentation.
