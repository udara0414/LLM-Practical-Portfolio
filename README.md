### Lab 02 – AI in Everyday Challenges

**Group 4 – The Dehydrated Student**

For this activity, our lecturer assigned different everyday challenges to each group. Our group was assigned the challenge **"The Dehydrated Student."**

Based on this challenge, we developed **Sipwise**, a proposed AI-assisted hydration support product designed for university students with busy academic schedules.

The product concept combines a timetable-aware mobile application, LLM-based functionalities, and a proposed ESP32-powered smart bottle cap.

**Key Areas:**
- Everyday problem analysis.
- LLM-assisted brainstorming and product development.
- AI-powered timetable interpretation.
- Personalized notification generation.
- Natural-language drink logging.
- Proposed IoT-based smart bottle cap.
- Visual product prototype.

**Product Tagline:**

"Drink water on your class schedule, not when you're already thirsty."
